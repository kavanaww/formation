About

Formed, then in formation

Formation means two things here, and they happen in order.

First, God forms you. Think basic training — the slow, honest work of the Word taking a life apart and putting it back together the way it was meant to be. Recovery of what was lost. Uncovering what was hidden. Discovering what God actually says.

Then, you get into formation. Nobody puts on the armor of God to stand alone. We lock shields with one another — a small, tight-knit church where people are known by name, scripture is opened every week, and nobody watches from the back row.

No stage. No smoke machines. An open Bible and a room of people who take it seriously.
