About

Formed, then in formation

Church isn't a building. It's a gathering — believers coming together to open the scriptures, be corrected, hold each other accountable, share honestly, and grow up in the faith.

The name came first, and it's never changed. Formation means two things, and they happen in order.

First, God forms you. You can't pour from an empty cup. Before you're any use to anyone else, you have to get right with God yourself — pull the plank out of your own eye, learn to put on the armor of God.

Then you get into formation. Whether we realize it or not, we're in a war. Our struggle isn't against flesh and blood (Ephesians 6:12). When soldiers get ready for battle, they get in formation — shoulder to shoulder, shields locked. A cord of three strands isn't quickly broken.

No stage. No smoke machines. An open Bible and a room of people who take it seriously.

The Process

Recover • Uncover • Discover

This isn't a slogan. It's the path we see God walk people down when it's done right.

Recover

from the damage of sin

“I will repay you for the years eaten by locusts—the swarming locust, the young locust, the destroying locust, and the devouring locust—My great army that I sent against you.” Joel 2:25

Sin takes things from us — hope, innocence, the sense that life means anything. When you hand your will and your life over to God, He doesn't just stop the bleeding. He restores what the locusts ate.

Uncover

your identity in Christ

“But whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away... And we, who with unveiled faces all reflect the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into His image with intensifying glory, which comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.” 2 Corinthians 3:16, 18
“Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away. Behold, the new has come!” 2 Corinthians 5:17

You can drop the big stuff and still not know who you are. So we ask the harder question: when you're stressed, angry or lonely, what are you still running to instead of God? Food, money, the phone at 2am. As those come off, the veil comes off — and you meet the person God actually made. Then you get to help someone else meet theirs.

Discover

His plan and purpose

“Build houses and settle down. Plant gardens and eat their produce... Seek the prosperity of the city to which I have sent you as exiles. Pray to the LORD on its behalf, for if it prospers, you too will prosper.” Jeremiah 29:5–7
“For I know the plans I have for you, declares the LORD, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, to give you a future and a hope.” Jeremiah 29:11
“You will seek Me and find Me when you search for Me with all your heart.” Jeremiah 29:13

The world runs on fear. God's answer to His people in exile wasn't run — it was build, plant, raise your kids, pray for your city. The more uncertain things get, the closer we stay to the One who holds the plans.

None of this happens on an island. It happens with people who will hear your confession and hold you to the truth. “Confess your sins to one another and pray for one another so that you may be healed” (James 5:16).
