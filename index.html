About

Formed, then in formation

Formation means two things here, and they happen in order.

First, God forms you. Think basic training — the slow, honest work of the Word taking a life apart and putting it back together the way it was meant to be. Recovery of what was lost. Uncovering what was hidden. Discovering what God actually says.

Then, you get into formation. Nobody puts on the armor of God to stand alone. We lock shields with one another — a small, tight-knit church where people are known by name, scripture is opened every week, and nobody watches from the back row.

No stage. No smoke machines. An open Bible and a room of people who take it seriously.

What a Sunday looks like

Nothing fancy, nothing rushed. Here's what you'll walk into.

  1. Worship

    We open by singing. No show — just people worshiping together.

  2. The Word

    Verse by verse through a book of the Bible. Right now we're working through Matthew. We dig into what the words actually mean, in plain language anyone can follow.

  3. Sharing

    After the message, the floor opens. Questions, pushback, what God is doing in your week — this is where it gets real.

  4. Communion

    We share the Lord's Supper together every week.

  5. Circle up

    We close by standing in a circle, holding hands, and praying over one another.

Come as you are. Email the pastor and we'll send you the time and place.

Pastor

Meet Pastor Moose

Timothy “Moose” Kavanagh · Founding Senior Pastor

Moose has spent more than thirteen years in the trenches with people — walking alongside men, women and teenagers as God rebuilds their lives. Formation grew out of that work: nine people in a living room with an open Bible in July 2025.

He preaches verse by verse, loves digging into the original words, and keeps it plain enough that anybody can follow. He's pursuing a master's in ministry at Lincoln Christian Seminary.

Moose and his wife Ashlie have five kids. Come on a Sunday and you'll meet the whole crew.
